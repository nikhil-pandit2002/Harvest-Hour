Certified Organic Farm · Kanakapura Road

Pick it. Cook it. Eat it within the hour.

Fourteen organic acres an hour south of Bengaluru. Harvest your own vegetables, cook them with our chef, and sit down to them while they are still warm from the field.

Survey No. 112/3, Harohalli, Kanakapura Road · 58 km from MG Road · Open all 7 days a week (Monday to Sunday)

Things to do

Pick your own

Out into the rows with a crate and a knife. A grower walks with you and explains why the methi is good this week and the tomatoes are two weeks away. You cut only what you want to eat.

Included · 75 minutes

Cook with the chef

You put your crate on the pass and the chef builds a menu around it, or you say what you feel like and he works out how to get there. Then you are on a station, not watching a demonstration.

Included · 90 minutes

The pottery shed

The same soil the vegetables come out of, on a wheel. Four wheels and a hand-building table, run by a potter from Channapatna. Children take their piece home fired the following week.

Rs 900 a person · 60 minutes

Farm school for children

Forty minutes on soil, seed and compost, pitched at five to twelve year olds and run by someone who likes children. They plant one thing, label it, and come back to find it grown.

Included for children · 40 minutes

Growing workshops

For adults who want the balcony to work. Composting, seed saving, a kitchen garden in four pots, and what actually goes wrong in a Bengaluru summer. Small groups, one topic a weekend.

Rs 1,200 a person · 90 minutes

Farm shop and crates

Take home what you could not eat, or put a crate on repeat and we deliver across Bengaluru on Saturdays. Members get first call on peak dates and two visits a year.

Crates from Rs 1,400 a month

How a Sunday runs

8:30

Arrival

A cold press of whatever is heaviest on the vine, and a walk through what is ready today.

9:15

Harvest

Into the rows with a crate. Children get their own smaller one and are generally more ruthless about it than their parents.

10:30

Split

Adults to the kitchen or the growing workshop. Children to farm school, then the pottery shed. Nobody is left holding a phone.

12:30

The long table

Served in courses at the pavilion, facing the field you cut it from two hours earlier.

14:00

The shop

Crates, preserves, jaggery from the next village, and a sourdough starter if you ask nicely.

The Long Table Pavilion

Twenty-five guests seated together facing the soil the vegetables left sixty minutes earlier.

Course 1 Served 12:30 PM

Why it is built this way

Organic on a label asks you to trust a certificate. It seemed simpler to let you cut the thing yourself. Everything else here follows from that one decision, including the parts that cost us money.
